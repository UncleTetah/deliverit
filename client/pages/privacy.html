Privacy Policy

At DeliverIt, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact information (name, email, phone number)
  • Account credentials
  • Delivery details and preferences
  • Payment information (processed securely through partners)
  • Location data for delivery tracking

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve our delivery services
  • Connect service seekers with delivery providers
  • Process payments securely
  • Communicate about your deliveries
  • Ensure the safety and security of our platform
  • Send important updates about our services

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Your data is encrypted and stored securely.

Your Rights

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
