How DeliverIt Works

Everything you need to know — for senders and for providers.

For Service Seekers

Getting your items delivered has never been easier with DeliverIt:

  1. Post a Delivery Request: Describe what you need delivered, specify pickup and delivery locations, and set your budget.
  2. Get Matched: Our system automatically connects you with verified providers in your area who have the right vehicle.
  3. Track in Real-Time: Once a provider accepts your delivery, track their progress on our interactive map.
  4. Safe & Secure: Items are insured during transit, and you can communicate directly with your provider.
  5. Confirm & Review: After successful delivery, confirm receipt and leave a review.

For Delivery Providers

Earn money by providing delivery services with DeliverIt:

  1. Create Your Profile: Register as a provider, add your vehicle details, and get verified.
  2. Browse Available Deliveries: View delivery requests in your area that match your vehicle type.
  3. Accept Deliveries: Choose deliveries that fit your schedule and earn competitive rates.
  4. Manage Your Route: Use optimized routing to complete multiple deliveries efficiently.
  5. Build Your Reputation: Earn positive reviews to unlock more opportunities and higher rates.

Our Safety Features

Your safety and security are our top priorities:

  • Verified Providers: All providers undergo thorough background checks.
  • Real-Time Tracking: Track your delivery every step of the way.
  • Secure Payments: All transactions are processed securely through our platform.
  • Insurance Coverage: All deliveries are covered by our insurance policy.
  • 24/7 Support: Our support team is always available to assist you.
  • Emergency Contact: Direct access to emergency services if needed.
