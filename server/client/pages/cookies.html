Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how DeliverIt uses cookies and similar technologies.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites work more efficiently and provide information to their owners.

How We Use Cookies

  • Authentication: To keep you logged in during your session.
  • Preferences: To remember your settings.
  • Analytics: To understand how visitors use our platform.
  • Security: To help detect and prevent fraudulent activity.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies: Required for the platform to function.
  • Performance cookies: Help us improve how DeliverIt works.
  • Functionality cookies: Remember your preferences.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our platform.
